Curriculum

One school, three stages of learning.

Play, inquiry, and the first steps into a second language.

Our primary years build a foundation of curiosity through inquiry-based units, not worksheets.

  • Inquiry-based units across science, social studies, and the arts
  • Second-language instruction begins in Pre-K
  • Daily outdoor learning block, rain or shine
  • Homeroom teachers loop with the same class for two years

Where independence starts to matter.

Middle schoolers take on real electives, real deadlines, and their first independent research project.

  • Elective wheel: coding, ceramics, journalism, and more each term
  • First independent research project in Grade 7
  • Advisory groups meet daily for goal-setting and support
  • Overnight expedition each spring, by grade level

College-ready, and ready for more than college.

An internationally benchmarked diploma track sits alongside a real capstone project every senior completes.

  • Internationally benchmarked diploma pathway
  • Senior capstone project, publicly presented each May
  • Dedicated college counseling from Grade 10 onward
  • Dual-enrollment options with two partner universities
Our Approach

Small classes. Real mentorship. Global outlook.

12:1 student-teacher ratio

Small enough that every student is known by name, in every subject, every year.

A truly global classroom

Students from 42 countries learn side by side — global perspective isn't a unit, it's daily life here.

Teachers who mentor, not just grade

Every teacher also advises, coaches, or runs a club — relationships extend past the classroom door.
