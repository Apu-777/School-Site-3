Thirty-four years, one guiding question.

Brightlands opened in 1992 with a simple question: what if a school treated the whole world as part of the syllabus? The campus has grown a great deal since — the question hasn't changed.

The Founding

Twelve families, a rented church hall, and one idea.

In 1992, a small group of expatriate and local families in Ellery Bay found themselves with the same problem: no nearby school taught the way they wanted their children taught, with real attention to other cultures and languages, not just a unit on it once a year.

They rented a church hall for two mornings a week and hired a single teacher, Margit Solheim, to run a mixed-age classroom of twelve students. Within three years, demand had outgrown the hall entirely.

Milestones

How the campus grew.

1992

Brightlands opens

Twelve students begin classes in a rented church hall, two mornings a week, under founding teacher Margit Solheim.

1997

First permanent campus

The school purchases its current site on Lakeview Terrace and opens with three classroom buildings.

2004

Senior grades added

Brightlands extends through Grade 12, graduating its first senior class in 2006.

2011

International diploma pathway

The school adopts an internationally benchmarked diploma track alongside its standard curriculum.

2018

Innovation Commons opens

A maker space, black-box theater, and student cafe open in a single new building, funded largely by alumni.

2025

Need-based aid expanded

Brightlands commits new endowment funds to covering up to 90% of tuition for qualifying families.

What Hasn't Changed

The same question, asked for 34 years.

Mixed-age foundations

Our Pre-K and Kindergarten classrooms still mix ages, just as they did in that first church hall.

Language from day one

A second language has been part of the very first year of school since 1992, not introduced later.

Built by the community

The Innovation Commons, like the original campus purchase, was funded largely by parents and alumni.

Brightlands International

An internationally minded Pre-K–12 school in Ellery Bay, teaching curious minds to think globally since 1992.
