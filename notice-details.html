Admissions Published August 28, 2026

Fall 2027 Applications Now Open

The application portal for the 2027–28 school year is now live for all grades, Pre-K through Grade 12. Families applying for Early Decision must submit a complete application, including transcripts and one teacher recommendation, by November 15, 2026. Early Decision applicants will receive a decision by December 12.

Regular Decision applications remain open through February 1, 2027, with decisions released by March 1. Financial aid applications are reviewed separately from admissions and can be submitted any time before your admissions decision — applying for aid never affects your child's admission outcome.

Required documents: completed application form, most recent two years of report cards, one teacher recommendation, and a short student reflection (Grades 6 and up only). A non-refundable $75 application fee applies; fee waivers are available on request.

Academics Published August 21, 2026

2026–27 Academic Calendar Published

The full academic calendar for the 2026–27 school year is now available in the parent portal. Highlights include:

Fall Term: September 2 – December 18, 2026, with a one-week break the week of November 23 for Thanksgiving.
Winter Term: January 5 – March 19, 2027, including mid-year exam week (January 26–30).
Spring Term: April 5 – June 11, 2027, ending with final exams and the Grade 12 graduation ceremony on June 12.

Two teacher in-service days (no classes for students) are scheduled for October 9 and February 12. A downloadable PDF version of the full calendar, including early-dismissal Fridays, is available from the front office or the parent portal.

Events Published August 14, 2026

Parent-Teacher Conference Schedule Released

Fall Parent-Teacher Conferences are scheduled for September 21 and 22, 2026. Sign-up slots open this Friday, August 28, at 9:00am through the parent portal, and are booked on a first-come, first-served basis.

Each conference is a 15-minute slot with your child's homeroom or advisory teacher. Families with children in multiple grades can request back-to-back slots where possible — note this in the "comments" field when booking. A virtual option over video call is available for families who cannot attend in person; select this when booking your slot.

If you're unable to find a slot that works, contact your child's teacher directly to arrange an alternate time.

Holiday Published August 5, 2026

Campus Closed for Labor Day

Brightlands campus will be closed on Monday, September 1, 2026 in observance of Labor Day. This closure includes all classes, the front office, before- and after-school care, and all athletics practices.

Regular classes and the normal school-day schedule resume on Tuesday, September 2. If your family has a scheduled campus tour or admissions appointment on September 1, our admissions office will reach out directly to reschedule.

Events Published August 8, 2026

Fall Sports Tryout Sign-Ups Open

Tryout sign-ups for our fall athletics season are now open. Soccer, swimming, and cross-country tryouts begin the week of September 8, 2026, and run after school through September 12.

Students trying out for any team must have a current physical exam on file with the school nurse — forms are available at the front office or in the parent portal under "Health Forms." Students without a physical on file will not be permitted to try out.

Tryout times and locations by sport will be posted outside the gymnasium and emailed to families the week before tryouts begin. Questions can be directed to the athletics office.
