Clubs & Activities

Clubs & Athletics

28 student-run clubs and 11 competitive teams, from Model UN and robotics to swimming and badminton. New clubs launch every term if five students and a faculty sponsor sign on.

The Global Table

Our cafeteria runs a monthly "Global Table" menu, cooked with recipes submitted by student families.

Outdoor Program

Every grade takes an annual expedition, from a day hike in Kindergarten to a week-long trip by senior year.

Student Council

An elected student council sits on the school's dining and facilities committees with a real vote, not an advisory one.
